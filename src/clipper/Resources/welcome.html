Glea Clipper is installed

It collects into Glea, the Mac app where your journal and notes live. Glea needs to be on this Mac for anything to arrive.

Download Glea

Already have it? Open it once so your browser knows about it.

Collecting

  • Hold ⌥ on a page to point and shoot: click a block to collect it, or drag to collect an area as a screenshot.
  • Click the toolbar button (⌥⇧G) for the selection, an article or a link.
  • Or right-click: Collect Selection / Image / Link / Page to Glea.

The first time, your browser asks whether to open Glea: allow it.
